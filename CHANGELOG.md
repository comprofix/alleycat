# Changelog

All notable changes to Alley Cat are listed here.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and the project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [1.3.0](https://github.com/comprofix/alleycat/compare/v1.2.1...v1.3.0) - 2026-10-26

### Added

- A new look, smooth art at 1280×800 drawn in code, and the one every
  player now starts on. The game underneath is unchanged: the same
  layout, timings, rules and sound. The pixel art of 1.2.1 is still
  there as the classic look, chosen under Options, Look.
- The alley: night sky, moon and skyline, lit windows behind curtains,
  laundry, trash cans and the fence. Freddy walks with a bob, flicking
  ears and a swinging tail; the dogs run on jointed legs with a wagging
  tail.
- The rooms: walls in perspective with wallpaper, windows, lamps and
  chairs, and each room's own things: the birdcage and canary, the
  bookcase and spider, the cheese and its mice, the sleeping dogs and
  their milk, the fishbowl and the water below. Things on the floor are
  drawn in the order they stand, so Freddy passes in front of or behind
  them.
- The heart room and the kiss, the title, and the tally, HERE KITTY,
  rejection, dancing cats and result words.
- Menus: a main menu (Play, Options, Help, Quit), a skill menu, options,
  a help screen listing the keys, and paws mode as a menu over the
  game, all with sounds. They work by keyboard, gamepad or mouse, and P
  pauses too.

### Changed

- The title now calls the game "reimagined".
- After a game, a key goes to the main menu rather than straight into
  another game.

## [1.2.1](https://github.com/comprofix/alleycat/compare/v1.2.0...v1.2.1) - 2026-10-25

### Fixed

- The mice in the alley and the caught mice at the top of the cheese room
  had a light line along the back and a dark one along the belly. They
  now catch the light on a few pixels of the back instead, as the cheese
  room's mice do.

## [1.2.0](https://github.com/comprofix/alleycat/compare/v1.1.1...v1.2.0) - 2026-10-25

### Changed

- A new look: the game moves from the Sweetie 16 palette to Resurrect 64,
  and every scene is shaded along its own ramp of colours, lit from the
  top left. Gameplay, timings and sound are unchanged.
- The alley: a dithered night sky, lit and shaded brick, lamp-lit windows
  behind curtains, shaded fence boards, cobbles and round trash cans.
- Freddy and every character, from the mice and the dogs to Felicia and
  the rival cats, are redrawn by hand with light and shade.
- The rooms: shaded walls, floorboards and furniture; the red rooms calmed
  to a deeper red; a shaded cheese wedge with deep holes.
- Underwater, Freddy and the eels keep their shading as the air runs low,
  and Freddy swims as the same cat seen in the alley, not the old longer
  figure. The sand and weed are shaded too.
- The heart room and the kiss: shaded hearts, cherubs and gifts, and the
  hearts keep their shading at the kiss.
- The title, tally and other screens: a shaded skyline and fence, and the
  name and the result words painted in bands of light and shade.
- The program icon's moon is shaded, and Freddy's ears match the game.

## [1.0.0](https://github.com/comprofix/alleycat/releases/tag/v1.0.0) - 2026-10-22

### Added

- A faithful remake of Alley Cat (1984 IBM PC version): Freddy in the
  alley, with the fences, trash cans, clotheslines and windows, thrown
  objects, mice, the trash-can cat and the bulldog.
- The five rooms: birdcage, cheese, fishbowl, sleeping dogs and bookshelf,
  then Felicia and the heart room, with the kiss bonus and the next level.
- Four skills, Kitten to Alley Cat, scoring, lives and the level timer.
- The original's tunes and PC-speaker-style sound effects, made in code.
- New art in the Sweetie 16 palette on the original's 320×200 screen,
  with square pixels or a 4:3 CRT shape, and fullscreen.
- Paws mode, sound on and off, restart and back to the skill menu.
- Settings and the high score saved beside the game.
- Release builds for Windows and Linux.
